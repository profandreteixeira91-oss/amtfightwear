# AMT Fight Wear & AMT Dojo Manager

A connected digital ecosystem designed for the martial arts market, combining **e-commerce, custom product management, academy management, payments, and operational workflows**.

This project was built with **AI-assisted development using Lovable**, with product requirements, business logic, workflows, system architecture, testing, and continuous improvements driven by real-world business needs.

# AMT Fight Wear

**AMT Fight Wear** is an e-commerce platform focused on martial arts products, including personalized belts, kimonos, rashguards, patches, and other training equipment.

The platform combines traditional e-commerce functionality with product customization and business-specific operational workflows.

### Key Features

* Product catalog and inventory management
* Personalized product ordering
* Custom product configuration
* Customer management
* Shopping cart and checkout
* Payment integration
* Wholesale sales workflows
* Pricing and margin management
* Order management
* Marketing and sales operations
* Fiscal and invoicing workflows
* Product and catalog administration

---

# AMT Dojo Manager

**AMT Dojo Manager** is a SaaS management platform designed for Brazilian martial arts academies.

The platform centralizes the main operational processes of a dojo into a single system.

### Key Features

* Student management
* Student profiles and documentation
* Attendance and check-in
* QR Code and PIN-based check-in
* Membership plans
* Recurring payments
* Financial management
* Graduation and belt history
* Class scheduling
* Lesson plans
* Competition management
* Evaluations
* Certificates
* Student app
* Academy administration
* Multi-unit / branch management
* Payment integrations
* Customer and communication workflows
